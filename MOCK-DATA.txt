สาน (Saan) installer demo provenance — 2026-11-05, full trial added 2026-11-13

The installer offers three kinds of initial data:
  demo   "พร้อมข้อมูลตัวอย่าง": the website sample below plus a tiny operational sample. No accounts besides the administrator.
  blank  "เว็บเปล่าพร้อมโครงหน้า": none of the fictional records/media; it keeps safe draft structures.
  full   "ทดลองเต็มระบบ (โรงเรียนสมมติ)": the website sample plus a whole fictional school in every module and trial accounts.
         For trials only. Never use a full-trial install for a real school.

WEBSITE SAMPLE (demo and full)
resources/install/demo is a curated export of the fictional สาน (Saan) demonstration.
62 published CMS records in 12 types, 53 referenced media assets. Each file is size/SHA-256 verified.
Only published settings and content ship. No source users/passwords/sessions/historical audit/private drafts.
Fictional operational samples (demo mode only): 6 teachers, 24 students, 3 classrooms, timetable/duty/attendance/leave examples.
No real identity numbers or scans, home-registration/citizen-card/passport images, or health records.

Artwork: monthly ChatGPT-authenticated Codex native image generation used for campus photos, fictional
adult staff portraits, category imagery, theme artwork and icon/banner sheets. Existing originals were
preserved in the developer archive; distributed assets are optimized derivatives. No paid API key needed
to install/use the CMS, and no image-generation service runs during installation.
Document covers: real first-page raster previews of generated fictional Thai example PDFs.
Videos: three 9-second still-image slideshows, no audio; not recorded school events.
Logos: original trusted book/stream/sprout SVG designs, three shapes, with transparent PNG alternatives.

FULL TRIAL (mode full, or `php artisan saan:demo install` on a blank trial that is still empty)
Code: app/Domain/Demo (DemoSchool and one class per area), command app/Console/Commands/SaanDemo.php.
The school "โรงเรียนต้นธารวิทยา" does not exist. It is built for the academic year and term of the install day:
  - two terms (the current one open, the one before it closed and its results announced), calendar days, 7 bell periods;
  - 13 staff (a principal and 12 teachers, six of them homeroom teachers), 6 classrooms (ม.1/1 to ม.6/1), 120 students,
    about 150 guardians, identity numbers of every kind the register knows (Thai, passport, G code, other, documents pending);
  - a clash-free full week of lessons per classroom, attendance of the last 15 school days, courses with scores (grades,
    GPA and GPAX in the student and guardian portals), behaviour, weight and height, health profiles;
  - personnel profiles and the public staff directory, leave requests in every state, a duty roster, payslips of one
    published period, fees (invoices, receipts, transfer slips waiting/approved/rejected), correspondence registers,
    assets and repair requests, an annual action plan with projects and reports;
  - clubs with roll calls and certificates, a finished student-council election, project-based learning, an exam with
    scored answer sheets, learning links, an archive of an older year, one API client and a request log;
  - on the website: group pages with documents, an open survey with answers, a closed quiz with certificates, a name list
    shown with masked family names, complaints in different states, contact messages, the 18 ITA items.
Everything is invented:
  - names are combinations made up for this data set (app/Domain/Demo/DemoNames.php); they are not taken from any list of
    real people, and a likeness to a living person is a coincidence;
  - Thai identity numbers pass the check digit so that the screens accept them, but all start with 0 9999 (a first digit
    the civil registry never issues); passports name the country code ZZ, which does not exist;
  - phone numbers use the prefix 000, addresses the invented ตำบลต้นธาร อำเภอธารทอง จังหวัดสมมติ, e-mail addresses the
    reserved domain example.test; links point to example.org;
  - payslip salaries are repeated digits (22,222.00 and so on) and every payslip item is labelled as a fictional figure;
  - documents and slips are generated placeholders (a one-page PDF with a line of Latin text, a drawn PNG), portraits are
    the six fictional staff portraits of the website sample. No scan, photo or record of a real person or school is used.
Accounts of the full trial:
  - the administrator is the account typed into the installer; nothing else has a known password before the install;
  - one account per kind of user is created (principal, homeroom teacher, subject teacher, finance officer, payroll
    officer, clerk/technician, website editor, two students, two guardians). Their passwords are random for every install,
    shown once on the installer's last page (or printed once by the command) and written to
    storage/app/private/demo-accounts.txt (mode 0600). Delete that file after noting the passwords. There is no fixed or
    documented password and no demo-login button; SCHOOL_DEMO stays false. The other teachers have accounts nobody knows
    the password of; the administrator can send them a password link;
  - the same list holds the generated room codes of two learning-link pages and the tracking code of one sample complaint.
A trial is marked: the school name carries "(ระบบทดลอง)", the site badge says so, the setting demo.school records the
build, storage/installed.json says mode "full" and `php artisan saan:demo status` reports it. `php artisan school:doctor`
and the page /cms#deployment show [WARN] "ระบบทดลอง (ข้อมูลสมมติ) ห้ามใช้กับโรงเรียนจริง" on such an install, and a second [WARN]
for as long as storage/app/private/demo-accounts.txt (the passwords) has not been deleted.
There is no uninstall. Throw the trial away (new folder, new database) and install blank for the real school.
